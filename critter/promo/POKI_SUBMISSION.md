## Title
Critter Vac

## Short description (one line)
Hold, aim and vacuum up adorable critters, then watch your park earn coins!

## Description
Grab your magic vacuum and head into the wild! Hold and aim to suck up adorable critters: fluffy bunnies, bees, slimes, owls and many more. Every critter has its own trick: fliers float up high, burrowers pop in and out of the ground, shy ones run from the wind, slimes split in two and snails hide in their shells.

Golden, rainbow and giant critters fight back: keep the wind on them until they give in. Catch them quickly in a row for a combo, and face the giant boss of every region.

Bring your catch to the Critter Park, where every species sits on its own pedestal and earns coins, even while you are away. Upgrade your suction, reach and jar, hatch surprise eggs, unlock the Mushroom Forest and the Frosty Peaks, and fill your critter index. Then race a friend: send a 60-second Rush challenge with a link!

## Controls
- Touch / mouse: hold and aim to vacuum. Tap buttons in the park.
- Keyboard: hold Space to vacuum, aim with the mouse.

## Notes for the playtest
- Ads only through the Poki SDK: a commercial break when heading back to the field from the park and before a Rush (never before the first play), rewarded videos only when the player taps a purple 🎬 button (golden lure, hatch an egg now).
- No external requests; saves in localStorage; offline park income capped at 2 hours.
- Works in portrait and landscape, mobile and desktop.
