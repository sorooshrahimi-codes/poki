# Deep Luck — the Poki funnel

One idea: **a little kid in a little boat hooks the giant Legend of the sea.** Every step from "sees the thumbnail" to "brings a friend" has one clear pull to the next.

## The story (wordless)
- **Start:** Captain Bo's logbook opens: five pages, five Legend silhouettes on a dotted sea road, and the kid's boat on the first page. Three seconds, tap to skip.
- **The road:** lake → ocean → frozen north → coral reef → volcano. Each sea has its Legend: Old Whiskers (catfish), Grand Marlin, Narwhal King, Coral Dragon, Phoenix Fish.
- **The goal you always see:** the line gauge on the left edge runs down toward the Legend's portrait at the bottom. Under the boat, the Legend's shadow lurks deep, rising and opening its eyes as your line gets closer.
- **The moment:** when the line reaches it, the Legend glows under the boat. The next cast goes for it: three rounds (hold the line, strike on time, follow it), with 👑 crowns for each round won.
- **After:** the logbook page lights up and the boat sails to the next sea. The harbour next to the boat grows (stand, lighthouse, trophy statue).
- **And then:** the Legends return stronger (★2, ★3...), so there is always a next goal.

## The funnel
| Step | What the player feels | What makes it happen |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Click | "That kid is fishing for THAT?" | The square thumbnail: a tiny boat and a giant catfish rising toward the hook. No text. The animated thumbnail adds the dive, the fight and the crown. |
| 2. First 5 seconds | "I get it" | The logbook (3 s, skippable), then a moving hand: tap to cast. The first cast always brings up a golden fish. |
| 3. First minute | "I'm getting stronger" | Catches fly up into the coin counter; a bouncing arrow points at the first upgrade; the line gauge fills; the shadow below starts to wake. |
| 4. Around 2 minutes | "WOW" | The first Legend: a three-round fight, a slow-motion crown moment, the logbook page lights up, a new sea opens. |
| 5. The session | "One more" | A Legend about every 2 minutes (simulator: 1:48, 3:23, 5:45, 7:49, 10:48). Each sea plays differently (ice slabs, currents, vents). Surprises: sharks, ink, crab, puffers, golden schools, chests. |
| 6. Coming back | "There's still more" | ★2 Legends, 53 creatures in the logbook, hats and boat paint to show off, weather and timed world events. |
| 7. Bringing friends | "Beat me!" | 🤝 after the first Legend: three casts in the same sea, then 📤 sends a link (`PokiSDK.shareableURL`) with your score to beat. |

## Fixed principles (same as our other games)
- **No words on the field.** Icons, colour and motion explain; the fish names appear only in the logbook.
- **The sea belongs to the player.** No panel after a dive: the catch pops over the boat and flies into the coins. The optional 🎬×2 waits at the side.
- **Few controls, at the edges.** The coins and the logbook sit top left; ⚙ top right (sound, language, pause in one tray); the Legend gauge on the left edge; the place and the wardrobe on the right edge; three upgrades at the bottom.
- **One line of progress:** fish → coins → upgrades → the Legend → the next sea.

## Our own look (not shared with our other games)
- **Sea Glass:** frosted, see-through glass bubbles with a highlight; deep-teal glows instead of dark outlines; coral for "go", aqua for "ok", purple glass for rewarded.
- **Font:** Baloo 2.
- **The logbook:** parchment pages and a dotted sea road, instead of a map screen.
