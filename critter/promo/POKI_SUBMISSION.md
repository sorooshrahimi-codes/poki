## Title
Critter Vac

## Short description (one line)
Vacuum up cute critters, power up, and suck in a GIANT!

## Description
A tiny kid. A magic vacuum. A giant the size of a hill.
Hold and aim to suck up adorable critters, grow your vacuum with every coin, and when the bar is full, the sleeping giant wakes up and the tug of war begins. Win, and it stretches like taffy straight into your nozzle!
Every giant you beat brings the colour back and opens a new place with a bigger giant. Collect every critter in your book, and race a friend in a 60-second Rush.

## Controls
- Touch / mouse: hold and aim to vacuum.
- Keyboard: hold Space to vacuum, aim with the mouse.

## Tags (suggested)

## Notes for the playtest
- Ads only through the Poki SDK: a commercial break after a giant is beaten and before a Rush (never before the first play); rewarded video only when the player taps the purple 🎬✨ button (golden critters).
- No text on the playing field; no external requests; saves in localStorage; offline income capped at 2 hours.
- Works in portrait and landscape, mobile and desktop. Build size about 1.9 MB.
