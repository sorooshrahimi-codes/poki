## Title
Deep Luck

## Short description (one line)
Cast your line, dodge the small fry and reel up legendary fish from five magical seas!

## Description
Captain Bo has left you his little boat and his old logbook. Fill it with every creature of the seas!

Drop your hook deep, steer around the small fish on the way down, then catch everything you can on the way up. Big fish fight back: hold, tap or follow to win the battle. Some fish come out golden, rainbow, ghostly or electric, and they are worth much more.

Explore a big world map: a peaceful lake, the open ocean, the Frozen North where ice blocks your hook, a Coral Reef with strong currents and a Volcano with erupting hot vents. Every place has three fishing spots, each with its own creatures.

Watch out for sharks that steal your catch, octopus ink, a crab that grabs your hook, and puffers that bounce you away. Upgrade your line, hooks and luck, keep fish in your aquarium, fill orders, dress up your fisher and boat, spin the Lucky Wheel, send your crew on trips, climb the weekly league and challenge a friend with a link!

## Controls
- Mouse / touch: tap to cast, move left and right to steer the hook, hold or tap during fights.
- Keyboard: Space to cast, arrow keys or A / D to steer, Space to reel during fights.

## Notes for the playtest
- Ads only through the Poki SDK: a commercial break between casts (never before the first cast), rewarded videos only when the player taps a purple 🎬 button.
- No external requests; everything is in the build. Saves in localStorage.
- Works in portrait and landscape, mobile and desktop.
- Languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Hindi, Japanese (auto-detected, switchable from the flag button).
