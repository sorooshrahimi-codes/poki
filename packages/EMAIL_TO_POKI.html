How we made them for Poki

We did not port existing games. We designed both from scratch for Poki:

  • Research first: we studied the genres that do well on Poki and its audience (kids and families, mostly on phones, playing in short sessions), and chose two themes that fit: fishing and a cute "suck it all up" game.
  • Readable in seconds: one finger, no reading needed, and a first reward within ten seconds.
  • Built for the platform: gameplayStart and gameplayStop, commercial breaks only at natural pauses, rewarded ads only when the player chooses, saved progress, small builds with relative paths.
  • Tested on phones and desktop browsers before submitting.
